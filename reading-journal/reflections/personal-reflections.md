# Personal Reflections

A collection of ideas that extend beyond individual books. These notes are personal and evolving that will be revised as I read, experience, and reconsider.

## Meaning: Discovery and Creation

I see meaning as a combination of what we discover and what we create. Experiences and relationships reveal what matters to us, while our choices and contributions give those discoveries direction.Meaning may not be a permanent answer. It can develop as our values, actions, relationships, and experiences change.

Reading and collecting information are only starting points. Understanding requires questioning, interpreting, connecting ideas, and forming conclusions of our own.

I want to move from consuming ideas to producing and testing my own thoughts.
